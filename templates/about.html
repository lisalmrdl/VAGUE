V.A.G.U.E.

Thanks for trying out V.A.G.U.E.!

This project was developed as part of the Building NLP Applications course at the University of Helsinki.

The goal was to understand and apply NLP and information retrieval strategies such as Boolean search, TF-IDF, and neural search, by implementing a simple search engine.

We also used data visualization and user experience (UX) design techniques to make the website more accessible and engaging.

The project was implemented in Python and relies on libraries such as Pandas, NumPy, Flask, Matplotlib, sqlite3, Sentence Transformers, and scikit-learn.
